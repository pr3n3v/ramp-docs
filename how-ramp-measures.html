01

What we measure

我们测什么

Four things, each stored separately. We do not collapse them into a single score, because a student who reads well and speaks poorly is a different student from one who does the reverse, and one number hides that.

  • Vocabulary词汇 Tracked one word at a time, in three states: unknown, recognised, producible. Recognising a word and being able to use it are recorded as different achievements.
  • Reading阅读 Speed and comprehension, measured on the texts the student actually reads, including ones they photograph or paste in themselves.
  • Writing写作 The student's own sentences, marked twice: once for the idea, once for the English. Version history is kept so a teacher can see how a piece changed.
  • Speaking口语 Pronunciation scored sound by sound against the standard, using Microsoft Azure Speech in the Singapore region.
4skills, stored separately
3states per word, from unknown to producible
1score we never produce: a single overall number
02

How we place a student at the start

入学分级

In two steps: a short adaptive check of vocabulary on the first day, and the rest of the check during the first week. First the student tells us where they think they are: five cards from A1 to C1, each with one line on what they can do, or 我不确定 / Not sure, which shows everyday statements to pick from instead. The quick check starts at the level they chose, or at A2 if they pick 还是不确定 / Still not sure. It moves up a level on a pass and down on a fail, and stops when it would return to a level already done, at either end of the scale, or after five levels. It is designed to take 2 to 4 minutes.

The kind of question changes with the level. Each level has its own pass mark.

  • A1 · Know the word认识单词 Common single words, one at a time. The student answers 认识 (I know it) or 不认识 (I don't know it). Twelve words: nine real, three made up to catch guessing. Pass: at least 7 of the 9 real words known, and at most 1 of the 3 made-up words claimed.
  • A2 · Fill the gap, with a Chinese hint填空，有中文提示 A sentence with one word missing. The first letter is given, with the Chinese meaning of the missing word, and the student types the word. Four sentences. Pass: 3 of 4.
  • B1 · Fill the gap填空 The same, with no Chinese hint. Four sentences. Pass: 3 of 4.
  • B2 and C1 · Finish the words补全单词 A short paragraph, everyday at B2 and academic at C1, in which some words have lost their second half. The student finishes each word. Eight blanks. Pass: 6 of 8.

Their level is the highest level they passed, or A1 if they passed none. Passing the know-the-word level alone can never place a student above A1: every level above it asks them to type the word. Nothing on screen says right or wrong during the check. Typed answers are marked exactly, except that one small typo is forgiven on words of five letters or more. It is never forgiven when it changes the letters we gave, or when it makes another real word or another form of the same word, such as a plural or a past tense, because those are the mistakes the product exists to find. Know-the-word answers never enter the student's word record: tapping 认识 is never taken as knowing a word.

We report how sure we are, and we say when we are not. If the check found a level the student passes and failed the level above it, we report confidence 0.8. If it ran off the top or the bottom of the scale, we never found their ceiling, and we report 0.5 with wording that says the check did not find out rather than pretending it did. The student is told the same thing on the result screen.

The rest of the check comes in the first week. A card on the student's home, 完成剩下的测试 / Finish the rest of your check, leads to timed reading, one piece of writing, and two speaking parts: reading aloud and a spoken explanation. Each part is saved when it is done, so it can be spread over several sittings. The written answer can confirm the quick check's level or move it one level up or down, and the record says which of the two decided it. When it moves the level, we report confidence 0.5. Reading and speaking are recorded separately and never count towards the level.

Marks are worked out and saved by the server. The phone sends only what the student typed or tapped. During the quick check it is not told whether an answer was right, and it can never mark its own check or write its own result. A database test proves that.

The student can see why, and can say it looks wrong. 为什么是这个水平 / Why this level shows each level in the order the student took it: how many they got right, the pass mark, and whether they passed. At A1 it shows real words known out of nine and made-up words claimed out of three, and never which words were made up. It never shows the right answers. Once the whole check is done, 这个结果好像不对？/ Does this result look wrong? lets the student ask their teacher to look, with a short note if they want. The teacher allows a retake, which sets the result aside, or keeps the result, and can reply either way. A retake draws on the same set of questions, so the student may see some of them again, and the teacher is told that before deciding.

The level picks where the course starts, and nothing else. The result screen puts the two side by side: 你说 B1 · 测出来 A2 / "You said B1. The check shows A2." The student starts at the checked level or, when the two differ, at the level they said. The course marks that unit 从这里开始 / Start here. Earlier units stay open, and no unit is ever locked. The English course has units up to B1 for now, so a higher level starts at the B1 units. A later full-check level does not move a start already chosen. The level never changes the Chinese help, which is decided word by word (section 3).

分级分两步：第一天做一个几分钟的词汇短测试，第一周里再做完剩下的部分。学生先说出自己觉得的水平（A1 到 C1 五张卡片，或者选「我不确定」），短测试就从这一级开始，还是不确定就从 A2 开始；过了升一级，没过降一级，最多五级。题型随等级变化：A1 判断认不认识单个单词，其中有几个是编出来的词，用来发现乱猜；A2 填空，给首字母和中文提示；B1 填空，只给首字母；B2 和 C1 在一小段文章里把只剩前一半的词补完整。每一级有自己的过关线，水平是学生过了的最高一级。测试过程中不显示对错；较长的词允许一个小的拼写错误，但复数、时态这类错误不算拼写错误。如果没有同时找到学生过得了的一级和过不了的上一级，我们会如实说明结果还不太稳。第一周里还要做计时阅读、一段写作和两部分口语；写作可以把水平上调或下调一级，阅读和口语不算进水平。所有判分都由服务器完成并保存，手机不能给自己判分。学生可以点「为什么是这个水平」看每一级的情况（不显示正确答案），全部做完后如果觉得结果不对，可以请老师看一看，老师可以让学生重做（重做用的还是同一套题，可能会遇到做过的题），也可以保留结果；两种情况老师都可以回复。水平只决定课程从哪一课开始：学生可以从测出来的水平开始，也可以从自己说的水平开始；不锁任何一课，也不改变任何一个词的中文提示。

03

How often we measure after that

之后多久测一次

Once more, in week ten, and otherwise never as an event. After the first week, assessment becomes a by-product of the work.

Every reading session, every unit, every piece of writing and every recording updates the record. Apart from week ten, a student is never asked to sit down and be examined again.

Week ten is a comparison. 63 days after the quick check, the same kind of check opens on a second form, B. Form B tests none of the words the first form tests and uses different paragraphs, and it starts at the level the first check started at, so the two walks can be compared. The rest of the check follows it again: reading, writing and speaking.

Help fades word by word, not by level. Each word a student meets carries its own scaffold: full Chinese gloss, then an English definition, then nothing. There is no global difficulty switch anywhere in the product, so they are never told they have been promoted or demoted as a person. Fading is judged over a seven-day window of their actual performance on that word.

Every failure comes back. Anything a student gets wrong is scheduled to return after 3 days, then 14, then 30, then 60, moving forward only when they get it right and resetting when they do not. Nothing is marked wrong without being scheduled to return.

除了第十周，分级之后不再有考试。评估变成学习的副产品：学生读的每一段、写的每一句、录的每一段音，都会更新记录。第十周是一次对比：短测试之后第 63 天，用第二套题（B 卷）再测一次。B 卷考的词和第一套题完全不同，短文也不一样；它从第一次开始的那一级开始，这样两次结果可以对比。之后同样要做阅读、写作和口语。中文提示按「词」逐个淡出，不按等级统一切换。

2checks, ever: the first week and week ten
63days from the quick check to the week-ten check
3 · 14 · 30 · 60days at which a failure returns

04

What happens when a student gets something wrong

答错之后

This is the part of the product we consider our own, and the part we would most like reviewed.

Every failure is diagnosed as language or idea. When a student studying in a second language gets a science question wrong, a conventional system records that they do not understand the science. Often they understood it and could not read the question. We label each failure language or concept, and only a concept failure counts against their understanding of the subject. If their wrong first answer took under five seconds and the second try was right, we are less sure it was the English, and the product treats it as possibly both.

The retry is clean. The student sees the same question again with Chinese support only. No hint, no extra time, no narrowing of the options. We record how long the first attempt took.

The feedback is short and it is theirs. It names the pattern in Chinese and quotes the student's own sentence back to them. It never returns a rewrite longer than one clause, and it never supplies the answer before they have attempted it.

学生做错时，我们会判断是「语言」还是「概念」的问题。只有概念问题才算作没掌握学科内容。重做时只给中文释义，不给任何提示，也不延长时间。

0hints on a retry
1clause, the longest correction we will write
05

The error patterns we can name

我们能识别的错误类型

A closed list of seven, of which six are built today. It is closed on purpose: a system that can name any error names too many, and a student who is told about eleven different mistakes learns nothing.

The first six are things Chinese does not mark on a word, which is where a Chinese-native student's written English most reliably breaks.

tense_verb_form third_person_s plural article copula pos_confusion pronoun_gender

The seventh is decided but not yet built. 他 and 她 are the same sound in Mandarin, so he/she confusion is the most characteristic Chinese-English error there is, and until 11 September 2026 we could not name it. Today nothing detects it. Naming it correctly requires working out who the pronoun refers to, which is harder than spotting a missing plural. So when it is built, it will be counted and shown to nobody, not the student, not the report, not the teacher, until we have measured how often we would accuse a student wrongly.

第七项（他/她混用）已经决定要做，但还没有做出来，目前不会检测。要判断代词指谁比判断复数少个 s 难得多，所以做出来以后也只统计、不展示：在我们测出误判率之前，不会对任何人显示。

1decided and not yet built. It stays silent until its false-alarm rate is measured

What we claim, and what we do not

我们承诺什么，不承诺什么

We claim this

  • We can tell you whether a failure was the English or the subject.我们能区分学生是没懂英语还是没懂学科。
  • We can show what a student can produce, not only what they recognise.我们看的是学生能写出什么，不只是认得什么。
  • We place each student, and we tell you how confident that placement is.我们给出等级，同时给出置信度。
  • Support fades per word, on evidence. A student is never told that less help means a new level.中文提示按词淡出，依据是学生的表现，不会把提示变少说成「升级」。
  • The level picks where the course starts. It never locks a unit and never changes the Chinese help.水平只决定课程从哪一课开始，不锁任何一课，也不改变中文提示。

We do not claim this

  • We are not an examination board. Our CEFR band is our own estimate, not a certified result.我们不是考试机构。等级是我们的估算，不是官方认证。
  • The quick check is a short vocabulary check, not a full measure of a student's English. It picks a starting point; the rest of the check adds reading, writing and speaking.短测试只测词汇，不能全面衡量学生的英语。它只用来找起点，第一周剩下的测试再补上阅读、写作和口语。
  • We do not issue an IELTS or Cambridge score.我们不出具雅思或剑桥成绩。
  • We do not replace a teacher. The product is built to hand a teacher one decision a day, not to take their job.我们不替代教师。产品每天只给教师一个判断，不接管教学。
  • We do not take a beginner to fluency. We take a student from unable to follow a lesson in English to able to follow one.我们做不到从零到流利。我们做的是让学生从听不懂英文课，到能听懂。

08

How we will check the diagnosis

我们怎样检验这个判断

Section 4 rests on one inference: if a student gets the question right once they are given the Chinese meanings of its hard words, the English was the problem. A teacher will see the weak point at once. Students often get a question right the second time simply because they have seen it before, with no help at all. If we only ever retried with Chinese, we would be giving the Chinese the credit for that.

So the check uses three kinds of retry, each on different items. In the product the retry always offers Chinese, as section 4 says. The other two kinds are used only in this check, so that the Chinese has something to be compared with.

  • Chinese meanings中文释义 Chinese meanings for four or five hard English words in the question, chosen before the student answers and printed beside it. In the product they tap a word to see its meaning; on paper we show them directly. 24 items.
  • No help什么都不给 The same question again, with nothing added. This shows how much a second look does on its own. 12 items.
  • English meanings英文释义 The same words, defined in simple English. This shows whether the help has to be in Chinese. 12 items.

Why test English meanings. The research does not settle which language of help works. A review of help given to English learners during tests (Kieffer, Rivera and Francis, 2012: twenty studies, over 9,400 English learners) found small but reliable gains from a few kinds of help, including an English glossary, and no reliable gain on average from help in the student's first language, though those results varied widely. The authors thought the most important factor may be the language the subject was taught in. A later review of 26 studies (Rios, Ihlenfeldt and Chavez, 2020) found no single kind of help with a reliable effect. All of these studies were of students in the United States. Most of ours read Chinese, which may favour Chinese help. They learn the subject in English, which, on that reasoning, may not. So we test it.

We do not choose which retry an item gets. A shuffled list, fixed and dated before any student answers, decides it: the seventh wrong answer gets the retry on line seven. If we chose, we would give the Chinese retry to the answers that already look like vocabulary problems, without meaning to, and the result would flatter us.

The pass mark. It is 70 percent agreement with the teacher on at least 20 items: 14 of 20, or 17 of 24 if all 24 count. It is counted only on the items that got Chinese meanings. Items the teacher marks "both", and items where the first answer turns out to have been right, are left out by rule, not by choice. We collect 24 so that at least 20 remain.

Two kinds of mistake, reported separately. Calling a failure language when the teacher says it was the idea is the worse one: the question still comes back to the student, as every failure does, but the idea behind it is not marked as weak, so the gap in their understanding can stay hidden. The reverse records them as weaker in the subject than they are, and the words that stopped them get no extra help. We treat that as the smaller mistake because the idea comes back to them, so they can show they know it. Our pass mark is still one combined figure, so we print the two kinds beside it, and a pass with many of the worse kind would still be a problem to fix.

What a check this size can and cannot show. It can show that the diagnosis is wrong. It cannot prove that it is right. The comparison is small too: twelve items each can show a large difference from the Chinese, not a small one, and the most likely result is that we cannot tell them apart. If so, we will say "cannot tell", not "no difference". The comparison does not decide the pass mark, but if the items with no help do about as well as the Chinese ones, we will say the check does not support the central claim, even if the pass mark is met. If it passes, we will say it survived a fair attempt to break it, not that it has been validated.

A weakness we have not solved. The same teacher decides whether each second answer was right, and our rule turns that one decision into "language" or "idea". The teacher then gives their own view of the cause, having already seen the second answer, so their view is not fully separate from ours, and that may make agreement easier to reach. This is one of the things we would most like your view on.

Two parts of this check already came from a teacher. An English teacher who read an earlier design of this check told us that a wrong answer on its own often does not show the cause, and that both problems can be present at once. So we now ask the student, in Chinese, how they were thinking, after the second try and never before it, because asking first would work as a hint. And when the teacher marking an item answers "both", they now say which they mean: they could see both problems in the answer, or they could not tell them apart. Neither is counted towards the pass mark, but we count how often each happens, because if both problems together turn out to be common, the product has to change.

第 4 节的判断依据是：学生答错后，看着中文释义把同一道题再做一次；做对了，就判为「语言」问题，仍然做错，就判为「概念」问题。这个推断有一个明显的弱点：学生第二次做同一道题，即使什么帮助都没有，也常常会做对。如果只用中文重做，这部分进步就会被算成中文的功劳。所以这项检验（目前尚未进行）用三种重做方式，每道题只用其中一种：给中文释义（24 题，即产品的做法）；什么都不给（12 题，看「再看一遍」本身有多大作用）；给英文释义（12 题，看是否一定要用中文。已有研究对此没有定论，而且我们的学生是用英文学习学科的，按这些研究的推理，这一点可能对中文不利）。每道题用哪一种，由一份事先打乱并存档的名单决定，而不是我们看了答案再挑。及格线是与教师判断七成一致（20 题中 14 题，或 24 题中 17 题），只计算给了中文释义的题。与教师的判断相比，系统可能在两个方向上判错，我们分别报告：教师认为是「概念」问题、系统却判为「语言」问题，这种错更严重，因为题目虽然还会回来，但学生在学科上的缺口不会被标记出来；反过来的错，会让学生在学科上被记得比实际更弱。这个规模的检验能够证明判断是错的，但不能证明它是对的；三种重做方式之间的比较，最可能的结果是看不出差别，届时我们会如实说明。还有一个我们尚未解决的问题：判断第二次作答是否正确的，和判断错因的，是同一位教师，因此这位教师的判断与系统的判断并不完全独立。

09

Student data, and who can see it

数据与可见范围

Speaking is solo. No student is ever shown another student's recording, pronunciation score or transcript. There are no leaderboards anywhere in the product, and this is enforced in the database rather than in the interface.

A student's voice. Recordings are stored in Cloudflare R2. During upload, parts of a recording sit in a cache for at most 15 minutes and are then deleted automatically, whether or not anything else happens.

Where it lives. The database is in Singapore, the speech service is in Singapore, and the application runs in Singapore. Nothing routes through the United States.

What a teacher sees. Records for students on the teacher's own roster only, enforced per row in the database. A teacher cannot open another teacher's class. On the Students page that includes each student's goal, the level they said, the level the quick check found, where they chose to start and the full-check level, and a Placement results to review list for any result a student has asked them to look at. One thing we would ask of you

Section 7 names our most important gap: the language-or-idea diagnosis has never been compared against a real teacher. Our own rule says it must agree with a teacher's judgement on at least 14 of 20 items before we build further on it.

If one of your teachers would mark the items for us, that check can be run. Section 8 explains the design. The teacher would need to know the subject and read Chinese. For each item they decide whether the student's second answer was right in the subject, whether the cause was the English, the idea, or both, and which words stopped the student, after hearing the student explain their thinking in Chinese. There are forty-eight items. We estimate about two and a half hours, but nobody has timed it yet. It needs no technical knowledge. We need the school's written permission before any student takes part, and a second teacher to mark at least five of the items separately.

It would turn the central claim in this document from a stated one into a tested one. We would publish the result either way, including if we fail it. And if you think the check in section 8 is unfair, in either direction, we would rather hear it before we run it than after.
